URBAN SPRAWL 1.0.0 preview 5

Start the game by running urban-sprawl (urban-sprawl.exe on Windows).

New town: pick Grid town or Winding town, keep or change the seed, press Start.
Then click one of the orange crossroads markers to place your town hall.

Controls
  Left drag or W A S D   move the camera
  Right drag             orbit
  Mouse wheel            zoom
  Q and E                rotate
  0, 1, 2, 3             pause, normal, 3x, 8x speed (Space toggles pause)
  Click a building       why it is the way it is
  F11                    fullscreen

The property tax slider is the one lever in this preview. Low taxes grow the town fast and
fill the treasury slowly. High taxes slow growth, and above 14 percent buildings empty out
and get torn down.

If the game does not start, the log is urban-sprawl.log in your temp folder.
